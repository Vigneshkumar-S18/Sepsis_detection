1. Executive Summary & Clinical Impact

Sepsis is a life-threatening organ dysfunction caused by a dysregulated host response to infection. In intensive care units (ICUs), every hour of delayed clinical recognition significantly increases mortality risk. **SEPESDETECTOR** is a state-of-the-art Clinical Decision Support System trained on 40,336 ICU patients (1.55M hourly records) from the PhysioNet 2019 Challenge dataset. The system engineered **391 clinical features** and trained deep temporal neural networks to issue alerts hours before clinical manifestation.

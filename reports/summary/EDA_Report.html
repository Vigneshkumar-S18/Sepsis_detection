6. Physiological Trajectories Leading to Sepsis (12 Hours Prior)

Aligning septic patient timelines by their diagnostic hour reveals a clear temporal degradation. Heart Rate and Respiration increase steadily, while Oxygen Saturation declines prior to onset, demonstrating the clinical value of time-series LSTM modeling.

7. Pipeline Preprocessing Conclusions

  • Imputation: Carry-forward (forward-fill) should be used for missing vital values up to a 6-hour threshold. Global mean imputation must be avoided to prevent variance dilution.
  • Robust Scaling: Outliers in blood pressure and heart rate require robust normalization strategies.
  • Class Weighted Loss: Models must incorporate Focal Loss or class weighting to counter the 98.20% class imbalance at the hourly sequence level.

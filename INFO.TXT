1. PROJECT OVERVIEW & CLINICAL SIGNIFICANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sepsis is a life-threatening organ dysfunction caused by a dysregulated host 
response to infection. If not recognized and treated early (e.g., via fluids and 
intravenous antibiotics), it rapidly progresses to septic shock, multi-organ failure, 
and death. In the ICU, every hour of delayed treatment increases mortality risk.

The THAARU Sepsis AI project is a state-of-the-art Clinical Decision Support System 
(CDSS) designed to predict sepsis onset hours before clinical manifestation. 
The system consolidates and processes patient vital signs, laboratory measurements, 
and demographic information to issue real-time early warnings, clinical suggestions, 
and localized explainability to bedside physicians and ICU nurses.

Dataset Summary:
- Source: PhysioNet Challenge 2019
- Unique Patients: 40,336
- Total Hourly Records: 1,552,210
- Class Imbalance: Sepsis is present in 1.80% of total hourly records.
- Septic Patient Prevalence: 7.27% of patients contract sepsis during their stay.
- Length of Stay: Average of 39.01 hours (range: 8 to 336 hours).


2. HOW THE SYSTEM AND MODEL NOW WORK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The project features a full loop from patient bedside monitoring to prediction, 
clinical alerting, explainability, and visual representation:

  +-----------------------+      Ingest      +-----------------------------+
  |  Patient Telemetry    | -------------->  | FastAPI Ingestion Endpoint  |
  |  (HR, Temp, labs...)  |                  | (server.py /patient/update) |
  +-----------------------+                  +-----------------------------+
                                                            |
                                                            v
  +-----------------------+   Read Buffer    +-----------------------------+
  | SQLite Database       | <--------------  | Rolling Window Manager      |
  | (sepsis_live.db)      |                  | (Up to 12h history)         |
  +-----------------------+                  +-----------------------------+
                                                            |
                                                            v
  +-----------------------+   Attributions   +-----------------------------+
  | PyTorch Captum XAI    | <--------------  | Online Preprocessor         |
  | (Integrated Gradients)|                  | (Feature Eng & scaling)     |
  +-----------------------+                  +-----------------------------+
              |                                             |
              v                                             v
  +------------------------------------------------------------------------+
  |                BiLSTM Classifier Sepsis Prediction Model                |
  |                   Outputs Sepsis Risk (e.g. 84.3%)                     |
  +------------------------------------------------------------------------+
              |                                             |
              v                                             v
  +-----------------------+                  +-----------------------------+
  | Alert Engine          |                  | Clinician Dashboard         |
  | (Critical, Rising, PP)|                  | (index.html / app.js)       |
  +-----------------------+                  +-----------------------------+

Detailed Execution Workflow:
1. Ingestion: Telemetry records containing patient vitals (Heart Rate, MAP, Temp, 
   SpO2, Resp, Blood Pressure) are sent via HTTP POST to `/api/patient/update`.
2. Database Storage: The server commits the telemetry records to `sepsis_live.db` 
   for persistence.
3. Rolling Window Queue: Upon query, the `RollingWindowBuffer` fetches the last 
   12 hours of stay for the patient (less if the patient just entered the ICU).
4. Preprocessing & Feature Engineering: The `OnlinePreprocessor` handles:
   - Laboratory sparsity flags: Generates flags (e.g. `Lactate_measured` = 1 
     if present, 0 otherwise) for sparse labs.
   - Imputation: Forward-fills missing values to carry over temporal persistence, 
     then fills remaining NaNs with clinical default values (e.g., HR = 80.0 bpm).
   - Clinical Feature Derivation: Computes 29 engineered features:
     * Cardiovascular Indices: Shock Index, Pulse Pressure, MAP deviation.
     * Clinical Ratios: HR/MAP, Resp/O2Sat, HR/Temp.
     * 6h Rolling Stats: Mean and Std/Variability of (HR, Resp, Temp, MAP, O2Sat).
     * 1h Lag Differences: Slopes of vital parameters to track acute worsening.
     * Lab Sparsity Timers: Tracks elapsed hours since Lactate, WBC, pH, etc. 
       were last ordered.
     * Stay Timeline Flags: Identifies first-24h stays and diurnal cycles.
   - Standardization: Standardizes features with `scaler.pkl` (fit on the Training 
     partition only).
   - Alignment: Reorders output columns to match the 391 features expected by the model.
5. Inference Engine: The sequence tensor of shape `(1, seq_len, 391)` is sent to 
   the trained PyTorch BiLSTM model. A sigmoid activation is applied to output 
   a sepsis risk probability (0 to 1).
6. Alert Generation: The `AlertEngine` compares the risk score against rules:
   - Critical Sepsis Warning: Sepsis risk >= 0.70.
   - Acute Worsening/Rising Risk: Sepsis risk increase >= 0.20 over past hour.
   - Persistent Danger Alert: Sepsis risk >= 0.80 for consecutive hours.
7. Explanations (Captum IG): The `OnlineIGExplainer` applies Integrated Gradients 
   to the patient's sequence trajectory. It identifies exactly which variables and 
   hours contributed to the risk score, returning them to the client.
8. Front-End Rendering: The clinician dashboard (`index.html` & `app.js`) polls 
   the API endpoints every 3 seconds, updating the live ward grid, displaying 
   alert notifications, drawing vitals trend lines, mapping temporal heatmaps, 
   and showing direct clinical suggestions.

  our initial architectures were already well-suited for the clinical features.


6. EXPLAINABILITY (XAI) INSIGHTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Global Explanations (XGBoost SHAP):
- Summary beeswarm plots show that length of ICU stay (`ICULOS`), temperature (`Temp`), 
  and laboratory measurement frequency (`hours_since_last_Lactate`, `hours_since_last_pH`) 
  dominate predictions. Hemodynamic ratios like `HR_MAP_Ratio` rank highly, 
  justifying feature engineering.

Local Explanations (BiLSTM Integrated Gradients):
- Temporal attribution heatmaps show that vitals in the last 2 hours of the stay 
  have the strongest contribution.
- Elevated Respiratory Rate (`Resp`) and Lactate levels in later hours contribute 
  the highest positive risk attribution, aligning with clinical guidelines.

Model Feature Concordance:
- Overlap of the top 20 features between XGBoost (SHAP) and BiLSTM (Integrated Gradients) 
  identified 11 matches, representing a Jaccard Similarity of 37.93%. 
  Concordant features include: `ICULOS`, `Temp`, `hours_since_last_Lactate`, 
  `hours_since_last_pH`, `Temp_roll_std_6h`, `WBC`, `Platelets`, and `Unit2`.

Clinical Error Profiles:
- False Negatives (FNs): Characterized by severe feature missingness (high percentage of 
  imputed Lactate, pH, and Creatinine). Model sensitivity drops on sparse charts.
- False Positives (FPs): Patients presenting with high heart rate, temperature, 
  and white blood cell counts. These align with SIRS (Systemic Inflammatory Response 
  Syndrome) criteria, causing false alarms in patients with inflammatory conditions 
  who do not develop sepsis.
